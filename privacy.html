Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. What we collect

When you create a WarmupPro account we collect the information you provide at sign-up:

  • Your name and email address (used as your login).
  • Your password, which is stored only as a salted hash — we never store or see your plain-text password.

When you connect an email account (Gmail, Google Workspace, or Outlook) we receive, through the official Google or Microsoft OAuth flow:

  • The connected mailbox's email address.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens that let us send and read mail on your behalf.

We do not ask for, and cannot see, your email passwords. Access is granted exclusively via OAuth.

2. How we use your information

We use the collected data only to operate the service you asked for:

  • To send and receive warm-up emails from and to your connected mailbox, using the Gmail API or Microsoft Graph API.
  • To measure deliverability (inbox vs. spam placement), manage labels/folders, and archive warm-up mail.
  • To keep your session signed in and to let you manage or disconnect your accounts.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your mailbox content for advertising or share it with third parties for their marketing.

3. How we protect your information

OAuth tokens and any credentials are encrypted at rest before being written to our database.

Communication between your browser and our servers uses HTTPS in production.

Access to your mailbox is limited to the specific permissions (scopes) you explicitly grant during the OAuth consent step, and only for the duration of your connection.

4. Third-party services

To access your mail we call the official Google (Gmail API) and Microsoft (Microsoft Graph) APIs. These providers process the mailbox data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We act as a separate OAuth client; Google and Microsoft do not receive your WarmupPro account details beyond what is required to service the API calls you initiate.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and connected-mailbox data for as long as your account is active. When you disconnect a mailbox, we stop using its tokens. When you delete your WarmupPro account, we delete your associated data, including stored tokens.

6. Your rights

You can review and disconnect any connected email account at any time from the Accounts page. You may request deletion of your account and its data by contacting us. Where required by law (for example, the GDPR or CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the application.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at rohit.dev.team@gmail.com.
